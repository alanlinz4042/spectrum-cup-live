Cup Standings

Each match: front half 1 pt · back half 1 pt · overall 1 pt. Top of the Rock is winner-takes-all (3 pts). Ties split. 72 points total.

Player Tally

Course Handicaps

Course Handicap = Index × (holes ÷ 18) × Slope ÷ 113 + (Rating − Par), rounded. These are full course handicaps. In every match, strokes are given relative to the lowest handicap in the group, so the low player (or low team) gets 0 and everyone else gets the difference; the scoring sheet shows that working for your match.

Handicap Index & GHIN

Admins update each player's current GHIN Handicap Index here before Wednesday; everything downstream recalculates. Send your index to Alan or your captain.

Mulligans

One per player for the event. $100: $50 to the GoFundMe, $50 to the winners' pot. Original shot must be on camera. Admins record mulligans and payments; three-putts are logged by players in Scoring.

Snake

Tap the 3P button on any hole in Scoring to log a three-putt. The last three-putt in a group holds the Snake; at round end the holder owes (three-putts in the group × players in the group × rate) to the pot.

On-Course Competitions

⚡ Props

Quick YES/NO calls fired from the Live tab — "Cobb makes this putt, $5". Each loser pays the stake; winners split it evenly. Creator or a captain settles.

Wagers

Dinner-table bets, on the record. Challenge a player; the wager waits until they accept on their phone, then it goes live and anyone can back a side. Odds come from the money on each side (winners split the pot in proportion to their stake). Either party or an admin settles it. Cash changes hands over Venmo, not here.
